# Product Concept: Myanmar Escrow Freelance Platform (Project Name: MM-Freelance-Escrow)

## Purpose & Vision
Currently, the freelance landscape in Myanmar lacks a secure, dedicated ecosystem. Following the closure of major platforms like Chate Sat and due to aggressive automated bans on global sites like Freelancer.com, Myanmar freelancers are left vulnerable to payment scams, while hiring managers risk paying for incomplete work. 

This platform bridges that gap. It is a B2C and B2B hybrid marketplace engineered specifically for Myanmar citizens. It offers an easy, low-barrier entry point for all skill levels (Beginner, Entry, Mid, Advanced, Pro) to earn income or secure local/regional talent without massive upfront costs, backed by a trusted, centralized manual escrow system.

## Core Pain Points Addressed
1. **Lack of Payment Protection (The Escrow Void):** Freelancers constantly get scammed out of their wages, and clients get ghosted after paying deposits.
2. **Global Platform Bans & Restrictions:** Heavy compliance blocks from Western platforms target Myanmar IPs and banking systems.
3. **Severe Local Currency & Banking Friction:** Complexities in cross-border payments require an architecture natively supporting local mobile wallets (KPay, WavePay) and digital assets (USDT).
4. **Language Barriers:** Most complex freelance UI/UX architectures are English-only, alienating emerging local talent.

## Core System Rules
*   **Role Mutability:** During Registration, a user *must* explicitly choose either **"Service Provider (Freelancer)"** or **"Service Receiver (Hiring)"**. This selection is immutable; it cannot be changed later.
*   **Monetization Structure:** Platform service fees are deducted upon project completion/milestone release:
    *   1% from the Hiring Manager's total budget.
    *   2% from the Freelancer's earned payout.
*   **Localization Default:** The default UI, instruction blocks, and legal templates are in **Burmese (Myanmar Language)**, with an easily accessible global toggle for **English**.
